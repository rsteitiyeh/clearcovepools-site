Pool deck repair costs $100 to $750 for a simple crack or spot fix, $750 to $2,500 for typical wood deck repair, and $1,500 to $10,800 for a full concrete resurfacing job covering 500 to 900 square feet. The number that fits your project depends on whether the deck needs a patch, a full resurface, or a complete tear-out, and that's a different decision tree than repairing the pool shell itself.

Pool deck repair and pool resurfacing get confused constantly, but they're separate projects with separate price tags. The deck is the walking surface around the water; the pool's interior surface (plaster, pebble, or tile) is a different job entirely, priced and repaired on its own schedule. This guide covers only the deck.

## How much does pool deck repair cost by project type?

| Repair type | Cost | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Simple crack or spot repair | $100 to $750 | Filling isolated cracks, patching small spalled areas |
| Wood deck repair (typical) | $750 to $2,500 | Board replacement, structural fixes on above-ground/semi-inground decks |
| Concrete resurfacing (full) | $1,500 to $10,800 | New overlay across 500 to 900 sq ft, basic to decorative finish |
| Full deck replacement | $25 to $55 per sq ft | Complete tear-out and rebuild, wood or concrete |

Figures reflect 2026 U.S. contractor-quote data tracked across resurfacing and deck-repair project categories ([HomeGuide, pool deck resurfacing cost, 2026](https://homeguide.com/costs/pool-deck-resurfacing-cost); [HomeGuide, concrete resurfacing cost, 2026](https://homeguide.com/costs/concrete-resurfacing-cost)).

## What does concrete pool deck resurfacing cost per square foot?

Concrete resurfacing runs $3 to $6 per square foot for a basic broom finish, $6 to $12 for a decorative stain or texture, and $7 to $20 for a stamped pattern replicating stone or tile. A national average project (500 to 900 square feet) totals $6,200, with a typical range of $1,500 to $10,800 depending on size and finish complexity ([HomeGuide, pool deck resurfacing cost, 2026](https://homeguide.com/costs/pool-deck-resurfacing-cost)).

**Worked example:** a 700-square-foot deck around a standard inground pool costs roughly $2,100 to $8,400 to resurface, landing near the middle of that range for a basic-to-decorative finish and toward the top for a full stamped overlay.

Kool Deck, the textured coating pool contractors use to keep concrete cool underfoot and add slip resistance, adds about $4 to $6 per square foot on top of a standard resurfacing job.

## When does a crack need a $200 patch versus a full resurface?

Cracks under about 1/4 inch wide, with no shifting or height difference between the two sides, are candidates for a simple patch: clean the crack, fill with a polymer-modified concrete filler, and seal. That's the $100 to $750 range.

Cracks wider than 1/4 inch, cracks that keep reappearing in the same spot, or any section of deck that's visibly heaved or sunken point to a foundation problem, not a surface one. Resurfacing over an unstable base just cracks again within a season or two. At that point the fix is leveling first (mudjacking runs $3 to $6 per square foot, polyurethane foam leveling runs $5 to $25 per square foot), then resurfacing on top of the now-stable slab.

## What determines whether you repair, resurface, or replace?

Repair or resurface concrete with only minor flaking or cracks under 1/4 inch and a stable base underneath. Replace when the damage is structural: large cracks, crumbling edges, or a slab that's shifted enough that a new overlay won't hold. A full concrete pool deck replacement costs $30 to $55 per square foot including demolition and disposal of the old material; a new pressure-treated wood deck runs $25 to $50 per square foot ([HomeGuide, pool deck resurfacing cost, 2026](https://homeguide.com/costs/pool-deck-resurfacing-cost)).

Wood, composite, or aluminum decks around above-ground and semi-inground pools follow a different repair path than concrete. Board-level repairs typically run $750 to $2,500, or as little as $100 to $750 for isolated board swaps. Stripping and repainting a wood deck costs $3 to $9 per square foot; staining alone runs $1.50 to $4.00 per square foot.

## What's included in the resurfacing process, and why does prep cost so much?

Concrete resurfacing is not just pouring a new layer. The standard sequence is power washing ($0.35 to $0.77 per square foot) to strip dirt, oils, and old coatings, then repairing or removing any unstable concrete, then leveling if the slab has settled, then pouring and finishing the new overlay, then sealing ($1 to $3 per square foot) to protect it. Skipping the prep steps is the single biggest reason a cheap resurfacing job fails within a year or two: a new overlay poured over a dirty or unstable base delaminates.

A properly prepped and sealed concrete resurfacing job lasts 8 to 15 years before it needs attention again ([HomeGuide, concrete resurfacing cost, 2026](https://homeguide.com/costs/concrete-resurfacing-cost)).

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does it cost to fix cracks in a pool deck?**
A simple crack repair, cleaning and filling a crack under 1/4 inch wide with no shifting, costs $100 to $750. Wider or recurring cracks usually mean an underlying base problem, which pushes the cost toward leveling plus resurfacing instead of a patch.

**Is it cheaper to repair or replace a pool deck?**
Repair or resurface is almost always cheaper, roughly $3 to $12 per square foot versus $25 to $55 per square foot for full replacement. Replacement only makes financial sense once the existing slab or wood structure is too damaged for a new surface to hold.

**How long does pool deck resurfacing last?**
A properly prepped and sealed concrete resurfacing job lasts 8 to 15 years. Wood deck refinishing lasts less, typically 3 to 7 years for a stain or paint job, depending on sun exposure and how well the wood was prepped.

**Can I resurface my pool deck myself?**
DIY concrete resurfacer is sold in 40-pound bags for $20 to $40, covering 15 to 80 square feet, and small crack repairs are within reach for most homeowners. Full resurfacing with color, stamping, or decorative texture is a two-person job at minimum and is where most DIY attempts go wrong on prep and timing.

Bottom line: budget $100 to $750 for a spot crack repair, $1,500 to $10,800 for full concrete resurfacing, and $25 to $55 per square foot if the deck needs a complete rebuild. The prep work, not the finish coat, is what determines whether the job lasts 10 years or falls apart in one.
